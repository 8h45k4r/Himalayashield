Rasuwa (Nepal–Tibet border) outburst flood

Event record 2026-08-26-rasuwa · record status ✱ UNVERIFIED · created 2026-08-28

This is a workbench, not a warning system. Every figure below carries its provenance; unverified means press-derived and presumed wrong in detail; null means deliberately not guessed.

Ice–rock avalanche high on the Nepal–Tibet border sent a flash flood of water, ice, rock and mud through valleys in Rasuwa district (Nepal) and adjacent Tibet. The collapse produced a seismic signal initially catalogued by USGS as a magnitude 4.4 earthquake and later reinterpreted as the avalanche itself.
