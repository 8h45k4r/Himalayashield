Glacial-lake watch

Tsho Rolpa (Rolwaling, Dolakha) ⊘ OFFLINE

Grew from 0.23 to 1.53 km² over five decades; level lowered by the government in 2000. The early warning system installed around 2000 is reported damaged and non-operational, and the area lacks a reliable mobile network. A lake called 'monitored' that is not. (source: PreventionWeb / The New Humanitarian reporting, 2026-08-28)

Imja Tsho (Solukhumbu) ✱ UNVERIFIED

Lowered 3.4 m in 2016 (Government of Nepal / UNDP / GEF); early warning system with automated sirens in six downstream settlements and SMS alerts along ~50 km of the Imja–Dudh Koshi installed. Current operational status NOT verified by this project — verification is the task, not the assumption. (source: EGU 2021 case study (Government of Nepal / UNDP programme reporting), 2026-08-28)
